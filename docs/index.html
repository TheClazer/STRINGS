STRINGS Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

STRINGS is a seated, hands-only VR game for Meta Quest, developed by Suchit SM.

What the app uses

Hand tracking, only to play the game. The position of your wrists and how far each finger is curled steer your puppet and trigger its moves. Hand data is processed on the headset in real time while you play. STRINGS does not store it or send it anywhere.

What it does not use

STRINGS does not use the microphone, the cameras or passthrough, room scanning, location, contacts, or any account or profile information.

What it stores

Your Puppet Workshop choices (body colour, hat colour and the rival settings you design) are saved on the headset so they are still there next time. Nothing else is saved, and you can remove this data by uninstalling the app.

What it sends

STRINGS's own code makes no network requests and sends no personal data to us or to anyone else. The app is built with the Unity engine and the Meta XR SDK. Those components, and the Meta Quest platform itself, may collect standard technical diagnostics (for example crash and performance data) under their own privacy policies: the Meta Privacy Policy and the Unity Privacy Policy.

Children

STRINGS is intended for users aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new date.
